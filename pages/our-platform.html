Loads Marketplace

Three load boards, direct shipper channels and tri-market rate forecasting — run through QuickManage AI, our transport management system.

Load Acquisition Workflow

  1. 1

    Source the freight

    DAT One, Truckstop and 123Loadboard, plus direct shipper and broker channels.

  2. 2

    Check the credit

    Broker solvency, QuickPay availability and accessorial schedules — before we negotiate.

  3. 3

    Negotiate the rate

    Peak rate-per-mile and fuel surcharge, backhaul pairing, pre-cleared detention and TONU.

  4. 4

    Present to you

    Matched to your lanes, target rate and home-time. You accept or decline — always your call.

  5. 5

    Lock the load

    Rate confirmation and broker packet signed under your limited power of attorney.

  6. 6

    Dispatch live

    Trilingual check-calls and customs coordination — no border dwell time.

  7. 7

    Claims & accessorials

    Detention, layover and TONU filed and chased on your behalf.

  8. 8

    You get paid

    Revenue direct to you or your factor. Our fee is invoiced weekly — 9% semis, 7% hotshots & box trucks.

The three boards we search

DAT One

The largest network in North America — 500,000+ loads a day, with RateView lane data to negotiate from a position of strength.

Truckstop

250,000+ loads a day — strongest for flatbed and hotshot freight, with broker vetting and credit scores.

123Loadboard

100,000+ loads a day — the value pick, with mileage, routing and toll calculations built into every search.
