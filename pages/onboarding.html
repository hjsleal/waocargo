Onboarding

Before the first load, every carrier provides the documents below. We verify them, sign the agreement together, and set you up — then you can take your first load. You accept or refuse every load — nothing is ever forced on you.

What we need from you

Signed dispatch service agreement Limited power of attorney MC authority (FMCSA-verified) + USDOT number Insurance certificate — $1,000,000 auto liability / $100,000 cargo Signed W-9 Factoring company + Notice of Assignment Driver CDL(s) Void cheque or bank letter (QuickPay / ACH) Cross-border: BOC-3 · UCR · PARS / PAPS · FAST

The insurance certificate must name Wao Cargo as certificate holder.

How it works

  1. Send your documents

    Through the carrier form or by email — tell us about your truck, your equipment and your lanes.

  2. We verify

    MC authority with FMCSA, insurance certificate and CDL status — before the first load.

  3. Sign the agreement

    Dispatch service agreement and limited power of attorney — no setup fees, no contract lock-in.

  4. We set you up

    Your carrier profile in QuickManage AI, factoring and QuickPay / ACH ready to go.

  5. First load

    You accept or refuse every load — nothing is ever forced on you. We handle the rest.

Ready to roll? Send your documents through the carrier form — we reply within one business day.

US carriers — what to send

  • Active FMCSA MC authority certificate + valid USDOT registration
  • COI — $1,000,000 auto / $100,000 cargo, naming Wao Cargo Inc. as certificate holder
  • Signed Form W-9
  • Factoring NOA letter + factoring contact info
  • Voided check or bank setup letter (direct deposit / ACH / QuickPay)
  • Valid CDL for all primary and assigned fleet drivers
  • BOC-3 filing (designation of process agents)
  • UCR registration (current calendar year)

Canada carriers — what to send

  • CTQ / NIR (Quebec), CVOR (Ontario) or NSC certificate — plus US DOT / MC for transborder haulers
  • COI — $1,000,000 auto / $100,000 cargo, naming Wao Cargo Inc., 1876 rue Gareau, Longueuil, QC
  • Workers' comp proof — CNESST (Quebec), WSIB (Ontario) or provincial equivalent
  • W-8BEN for Canadian entities (or W-9 for US branches)
  • Factoring NOA or direct billing contacts
  • Voided cheque or bank verification letter (EFT / ACH / QuickPay)
  • Valid Class 1 licence for all assigned drivers
  • Cross-border readiness — FAST / TWIC status, PARS / PAPS numbers, e-Manifest readiness (ACE / ACI)

The 4-step onboarding workflow

  1. 1

    Digital packet

    Submit your core credentials through our secure partner portal — auto-fill packet routing, no paper-heavy onboarding.

  2. 2

    Compliance & safety audit

    FMCSA and SaferWatch validation, plus cross-border customs readiness (FAST, C-TPAT, SAT / Carta Porte).

  3. 3

    Lane profiling & rate strategy

    A brief alignment session: your headhaul and backhaul corridors, target rate-per-mile, home-time and max deadhead radius.

  4. 4

    Dispatch activation

    A dedicated bilingual dispatcher on phone, text and WhatsApp; your first load locked with pre-cleared accessorials and 24/7 support.
