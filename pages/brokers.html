  • Credit Sheet & References — billing details, trade references, surety bond BMC-84/85, payment terms
  • Master Service Agreement (MSA)
  • Accessorial Fee Schedule — detention thresholds, layover rates, TONU terms
  • Tracking & Visibility Specs — MacroPoint, Trucker Tools, project44, or check-call routines
