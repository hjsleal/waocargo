Regulations

Carriers running in the USA need active MC authority, a USDOT number, federal minimum insurance, a BOC-3 filing and UCR registration. Canadian carriers running south also use PARS (into Canada) and PAPS (into the USA), plus FAST where it helps. WAO CARGO verifies these items before the first load — see Onboarding.
