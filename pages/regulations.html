Data protection

Personal data is handled under PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25, with US state laws where they apply. Profiling and tracking stay off by default, transfers outside Quebec or Canada go through a privacy impact assessment, and data travels over TLS/SSL and rests in encrypted storage hosted in Canada.

Contracts & claims

Cargo claims are settled directly between the carrier, the broker or shipper and the insurers — under the Carmack Amendment (US) or the Provincial Carriage of Goods Acts (Canada). The limited power of attorney covers routine administration only: signing broker packets, accepting approved rate confirmations, and filing detention and TONU claims. Governing law: Province of Quebec, exclusive jurisdiction Longueuil.
