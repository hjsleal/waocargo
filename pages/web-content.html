Governance

How waocargo.com is run, hosted and protected — and what Wao Cargo is, and is not. This page states the rules we operate under.

domain

One official site

waocargo.com is our sole official web property. Alternative domain purchases and migrations are excluded.

cloud

Cloudflare Pages + GitHub

A static site — HTML, CSS and JavaScript only — deployed from the hjsleal/waocargo repository, main branch, with active SSL.

language

Trilingual by design

Every page in English, Spanish and French. In French, “Broker” reads “Courtier”.

lock

Privacy first

Quebec Law 25, PIPEDA and applicable US state laws. Non-essential cookies stay off until you accept — and the Meta Pixel only fires with your consent.

shield

Security

TLS/SSL on every public endpoint, MFA and role-based access for administration, encrypted cloud storage.

gavel

Clear limits

An independent dispatch service — not a motor carrier, freight forwarder or broker. No equipment, no cargo custody, no forced dispatch.

Your rights over your data

Access & rectificationPortabilityDe-indexing & erasureHuman review of automated decisionsNo discrimination for exercising rights

Write to compliance@waocargo.com — we answer in writing within 30 days. Privacy Officer: Hernan Sanchez.

Liability, plainly. The site is provided “as is”. Our liability is capped at the dispatch fees actually paid in the month before a claim, and cargo claims are settled between the carrier, the shipper or broker and the insurers — never by Wao Cargo.

Service descriptions and pricing on this site are the current offer; the signed dispatch service agreement governs the relationship.
