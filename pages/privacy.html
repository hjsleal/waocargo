Privacy Policy

Wao Cargo Inc. (“WAO CARGO”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and what rights you have under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25).

1. Information we collect

We collect the information you give us: name, phone number, email address, equipment details, operating authority and insurance documents, and the contents of messages you send us through the site or by email, phone or WhatsApp. We also collect basic technical data such as pages visited.

2. How we use it

We use your information to provide dispatch services, find and book loads, prepare broker packets, check broker credit, handle billing and detention claims, and communicate with you in English, Spanish or French. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

We share your information only as needed to serve you: with brokers and shippers for load bookings, with our transport management system (QuickManage AI) and load boards (DAT, Truckstop, 123Loadboard), and with service providers who help us operate. We never share it for marketing by third parties.

4. Cookies and tracking

Our site may use cookies and, once installed, the Meta Pixel to measure visits and advertising. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

5. Retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we protect it with reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your rights

Under PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent, by writing to contact@waocargo.com. We will respond within the time required by law. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version is always on this page. Questions about privacy: contact@waocargo.com, 1876 rue Gareau, Longueuil, QC J4N 1E4, Canada.

7. Cambios y contacto

Podemos actualizar esta política de vez en cuando; la versión vigente siempre está en esta página. Preguntas sobre privacidad: contact@waocargo.com, 1876 rue Gareau, Longueuil, QC J4N 1E4, Canada.
