To carriers

You drive. We find the loads, negotiate the rate, set up the broker packets, check broker credit, chase detention and layover pay, and help with billing. Equipment we serve: hotshots (35–40 ft), box trucks, dry vans, flatbeds and reefers — in the USA and Canada, in English, Spanish or French. Pricing: 9% of gross per load for semis, 7% for hotshots and box trucks. No setup fees, no contract, no forced dispatch.
