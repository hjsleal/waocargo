To carriers

You drive. We find the loads, negotiate the rate and handle the back office — and you accept or refuse every load. Nothing is ever forced on you.

How working with us flows

  1. 1

    We find the load

    DAT One, Truckstop and 123Loadboard, plus direct shipper channels — matched to your lanes and target rate.

  2. 2

    We negotiate the rate

    Peak rate-per-mile and fuel surcharge, with detention, layover and TONU terms cleared before you accept.

  3. 3

    You decide

    Full load details — pickup, delivery, weight, gross revenue. Accept or decline, always your call.

  4. 4

    We book & track

    Broker packet and rate confirmation under your power of attorney, then check-calls and live tracking.

  5. 5

    You get paid

    BOL, POD and billing handled — revenue paid directly to you or your factoring company.

What we need from you

  • Active authority — FMCSA (US DOT / MC), CTQ, CVOR or SCT
  • $1,000,000 auto / $100,000 cargo insurance, naming Wao Cargo
  • Hours of Service + ELD, valid CDL / Class 1
  • Routine vehicle safety inspections (DOT / MTO)
  • W-9 (US) or W-8BEN (Canada / Mexico)
  • Notice of Assignment + banking / factoring setup
  • BOL / POD, scale tickets and detention receipts for billing

Your money stays yours. Freight revenue is paid directly to you or your factoring company — Wao Cargo never holds carrier funds and never guarantees a broker's solvency.
