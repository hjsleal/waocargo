Payments & factoring

Payments

Freight money flows straight to you — never through us. We handle the paperwork, the factoring coordination and the accessorial claims, and our fee is a simple percentage of gross.

From delivery to payday

  1. 1

    Deliver & sign the POD

    Signed proof of delivery at the dock — the load is done.

  2. 2

    We submit the file

    Rate confirmation, signed BOL/POD and invoice go to the broker or your factor.

  3. 3

    Advance the same day

    Your factor advances 90–97% of the invoice, usually within 24 hours.

  4. 4

    Broker pays the factor

    Brokers normally pay in 30–60 days — the wait is theirs, not yours.

  5. 5

    Reserve released

    The balance reaches you, minus the factoring fee.

Factoring, explained

payments

Same-day cash

Sell the invoice and get most of it today, instead of waiting 30 to 60 days for the broker to pay.

percent

Typical cost 1.5–5%

Usually 2–3% per invoice for owner-operators — a flat fee, not a loan, with no monthly payments.

verified

Approved on the broker's credit

Your broker's creditworthiness decides — not your personal credit score. New authorities routinely qualify.

local_shipping

Fuel advances

Cash at pickup to cover diesel, tolls and lumpers — before the load is even delivered.

receipt_long

Three documents

Rate confirmation, signed BOL or POD, and the invoice — that is the whole funding file.

shield

Recourse vs non-recourse

Recourse costs less; non-recourse shifts the risk of a broker default to the factor.

Our fee — simple and weekly

9%

9% of gross — semis

7%

7% of gross — hotshots & box trucks

Weekly service invoices, due on receipt or by automatic deduction from your factoring account. Late charge: 1.5% per month (19.56% per year). If an agreement ends while a shipment is in transit, the delivery must still be completed and all dispatch fees remain due.
