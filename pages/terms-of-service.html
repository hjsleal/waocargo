1. Our services

Wao Cargo provides load dispatching and carrier support for owner-operators and small fleets in the United States and Canada, including load search and booking, rate negotiation, broker packets, broker credit checks, billing assistance and detention claims. We act as an independent dispatch service. We are not a broker, not a carrier and not an employer, and we do not transport freight ourselves.

2. Fees

Our fee is 9% of gross revenue per load for semis (dry van, reefer, flatbed) and 7% for hotshots and box trucks. There are no setup fees and no long-term contract. Fees are invoiced per load as set out in the dispatch service agreement signed with each carrier. The signed agreement governs if it differs from this website.

3. No forced dispatch

You accept or refuse every load. Nothing is ever forced on you. We present loads and rates; the final decision to accept a load is always yours.

4. Carrier obligations

You are responsible for maintaining valid operating authority, insurance, licences and equipment, and for complying with all applicable laws and regulations in the United States and Canada, including hours-of-service rules. You must provide accurate information about your equipment, availability and documents.

5. Payments

Fees are due as invoiced under your dispatch service agreement. Late payment may result in suspension of dispatch services until the balance is settled. Any dispute about an invoice must be raised within 15 days of the invoice date.

6. Cancellation

You may cancel our services at any time by written notice. Fees already earned on loads booked before cancellation remain due. Loads already accepted must still be completed or cancelled with the broker under the applicable terms.

7. Liability

Our liability is limited to the fees paid for the load concerned. We are not liable for broker non-payment, delays, cargo claims, fines or lost revenue. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law.
