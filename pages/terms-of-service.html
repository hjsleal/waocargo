1. Our services

WAO CARGO provides load dispatching and carrier support for owner-operators and small fleets in the United States and Canada, including load search and booking, rate negotiation, broker packets, broker credit checks, billing assistance and detention claims. 2. Fees

Our fee is 9% of gross revenue per load for semis (dry van, reefer, flatbed) and 7% for hotshots and box trucks. There are no setup fees and no long-term contract. 3. No forced dispatch

Fees are due as invoiced under your dispatch service agreement. Late payment may result in suspension of dispatch services until the balance is settled. Any dispute about an invoice must be raised within 15 days of the invoice date.

6. Cancellation

You may cancel our services at any time by written notice. Fees already earned on loads booked before cancellation remain due. Loads already accepted must still be completed or cancelled with the broker under the applicable terms.
